Good to know

Which wallets count as holders?

We take a snapshot of every holder of each collection on Robinhood Chain. If your wallet held the NFT when the snapshot was taken, you can claim. NFTs listed on a marketplace still count if they stayed in your wallet.

Can I claim more than once?

Each holding wallet can claim once per collection, and each claim wallet can only receive one GTD spot.

Do I need to sign or pay anything?

No. Nothing is connected and nothing is signed. Never share your seed phrase or private key; we will never ask for them.

When is the mint?

Follow @niblekins on X for the date. Your claim wallet will be on the GTD list.
